Can it solve cubic and quartic equations?
Yes. Higher-degree polynomial equations are solved numerically and accepted only after a residual check.
Does it solve systems of equations?
Yes. Put each equation on a separate line in the Advanced Solver.
Can it find numerical roots?
Yes. Numerical root workflows are available for equations that need an initial guess or bracket.
